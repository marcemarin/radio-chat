# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: the **producer** of a live radio show (Argentina first; pilot with a local FM in Posadas). During the broadcast they sit at a 13–15" notebook next to the studio, under normal light, at arm's length, with the show's WhatsApp open beside them. They read fast, in bursts, while the show is on air; their job is to decide in seconds which listener messages deserve to go on air, and hand them to the host.

Secondary: the **host**, who sees only what the producer chose (own screen, `/aire`). In small stations one person plays both roles.

## Product Purpose

Radio Chat receives the audience's WhatsApp messages during a live show, transcribes the voice notes (most messages are audio, and audio cannot be skimmed), classifies each one (intent, tone, on-air score, moderation, topic, place, name) and streams them to a live board where the producer queues the best ones for the host. Success: the producer stops scrolling a phone, finds the good messages within seconds of arrival, and more listener voices get on air.

## Positioning

Built for the burst: hundreds of voice notes an hour during a show, then nothing. Transcription plus a decision model make every voice note readable and ranked about a second after it arrives; an LLM is spent only on messages worth it. WhatsApp broadcast tools and CRMs solve the opposite problem (sending); nothing in the market triages inbound audio for live radio.

## Operating Context

- Two screens: `/` for the producer (feed, filters, topics, queue) and `/aire` for the host (what is on air, next up).
- Messages arrive via a WhatsApp webhook; each one shows immediately, then fills in: "descargando audio" → "transcribiendo" → classified. Latency to usable is ~10–15 s for a voice note.
- The producer's actions: play a voice note, read its transcript, send it to the queue ("Al aire"), reorder or discard queued items, put one on air, mark it as aired, flag a bad transcription, ignore spam.
- Volume for a local FM: 300–800 messages a day, 60% voice notes of 15–25 s. For big shows: thousands per hour.
- Language of the UI and content: Rioplatense Spanish. English only in code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Message types: text, audio, image, video, document; groups are out of scope.
- Classification fields: intent (reclamo, pedido_musical, saludo, opinion, concurso, consulta, spam, otro), sentiment, on_air_score 0–100, moderation flags (insulto, spam, datos_personales) with an uncertainty band surfaced as "revisar", topic label, location, listener name, one-line summary. Confidence per decision is available.
- Queue states: pending → on_air → done | discarded. One item on air at a time per program.
- Realtime via websockets; the board never polls.
- Stack: Laravel 13, React 19, Vite, Tailwind 4. No design system yet.
- Undecided: authentication and multi-program support (single program per install today); a per-station theme (each radio's colors) is a possible future need, not a commitment.

## Product Principles

1. Decide in seconds: the good message must be findable at a glance while the show is on air.
2. Audio first: a voice note is a first-class item, playable in one click, never buried behind its transcript.
3. Show uncertainty instead of hiding it: a doubtful moderation call is a "revisar", not a silent decision.
4. It is live software, not a document: state changes (new, transcribing, queued, on air, aired) are visible as they happen.
5. Nothing that needs training: a producer should use it on first contact, with no configuration.
